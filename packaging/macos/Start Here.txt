HP LaserJet 1020 Plus — installation for your Mac

1. Copy HP-LaserJet-1020-Plus-1.0.0-Apple-Silicon.pkg to your dad's Mac.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer. Enter his Mac administrator password
   when asked. Nothing else needs downloading or installing.
3. Connect the printer to his Mac by USB and turn it on. Use a USB adapter if
   needed. If macOS asks to allow the accessory, choose Allow.
4. Open a document, choose Print, and select HP LaserJet 1020 Plus.
   Start with one page.

This is for Apple M-series Macs, not Intel Macs. It uses the working HP firmware
and foo2zjs setup. It does not use the unfinished replacement firmware.

If macOS blocks the installer because it cannot verify the developer:
After trying to open it, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to
the security message, and choose Open Anyway for this installer. Then open it
again. This private package is not signed with an Apple Developer ID or notarized.
Do not disable your Mac's general security settings.
Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/102445

The printer must be connected directly to the Mac that is printing. This package
does not add Wi-Fi. Aayush's Mac does not need to remain on.

After power-off, the first job reloads the printer's original firmware. Allow a
little extra time. Supported choices: A4 or US Letter, black and white, copies.
Automatic double-sided printing is not supported.

If a job disappears from Print Center but no page appears, check the USB cable,
power and paper. Check whether any pages came out before submitting it again.
The package never automatically repeats a job after an uncertain USB result.

To remove this packaged setup later, double-click
Remove-HP-LaserJet-1020-Plus.pkg. Finish printing first; removal also deletes its
pending and retained failed documents. It does not remove other printers.

This installer has been built and checked without contacting a printer. Installing
and printing a page on your dad's Mac remain the final compatibility check.
Keep this as a private family copy.
