## Return, ownership and storage evidence

The four WAIT and two STALE rows must match actual function entries, semantic
arguments, return values and bounded instruction/access history. Enum domains
and identity domains are distinct. A row alone cannot prove that a call occurred
or returned that value. Decode original cookie/ticket arguments using the audited
target call0 ABI, including aggregate padding, rather than assuming endpoint is
an entire raw register. No candidate helper may manufacture an expected return.

All four WAITs preserve production objects/buffers and recovery permission, apart
from adapter.last_class_result and transient enter/leave busy stores. Successful
promises use the same original ticket and are counted separately. The two stale
calls preserve all production objects, private core, setup memory, descriptor,
payload, and acquisition last/first diagnostics; no hook or controller-log row is
allowed. Provider check rows/counters and ordinary call-stack bytes may change.
The three paired reuse snapshots must show identical production bytes and original
current owner across both stale calls, before ram_install_bulk writes anything.

Exact slot1 CPU and DMA reuse must connect old bind7 to new bind10 independently
of scope labels and history rows. Require the original saved cookie in actual
arguments and owner fields. The late old SUCCESS32 may write its legitimately
owned CPU prefix and retire the component; it cannot supply a recovery promise,
admit receive completion(2,6), feed the old tail, release its unready reservation
or deliver old output. Actual callback drain precedes successful RECEIVE.

The final healthy ZLP is separate from cancellation. At first close retain
cookie(15,4,3,7,1). Do not service between close and final acquisition. Pre-final-
service must show PENDING/count1/consumed6/core05; pre-finish must show owner FREE,
count0/consumed7/core00. The one event(3,1,0,1) and32 FF bytes precede close/finish;
second pixel/event capacity stays zero. None is produced by EOF.
